## Inquiry flow
1. Visitor submits a **prepared email** through the landing page, or messages "DESIGN" on a connected social platform. The current form uses mailto: it does NOT transmit to a server, create a record, or send email without the visitor pressing Send in their email application.
2. Review replies in deaconjason@medisyncdtechnologies.com; Gmail label: `MediSyncD Creative Studio / Leads`.
3. Qualify fit: non-sensitive work; clear deadline; client can provide all content and usage rights; no ongoing maintenance; no personal medical/child information; no high-risk regulated or clinical functionality; within available capacity.
4. Request missing scope once; issue a **written quote** with deliverables, exact price, due date start trigger, two consolidated revisions, exclusions, any third-party license costs, client review deadlines and payment terms.
5. Execute signed service agreement and SOW. Send an itemized PayPal invoice/deposit request through the approved business PayPal account; never invent a payment URL.
6. Mark project **Booked** ONLY when signed agreement and deposit both confirmed. If either is missing, status remains **Qualified / Pending**.
7. Stop accepting deposits when two projects are booked; inform further inquiries of the next available estimate or waitlist after consent. No false scarcity claims.

## Scope limits / safeguards
- No unlimited revisions. Two **consolidated** rounds: each round is one complete written feedback set.
- Changes after scope approval require a signed change order and price/timeline update.
- Turnaround starts after agreement, deposit and required client materials.
- Website quote excludes hosting fees, domain registration, premium assets, hosting management, security monitoring, complex web apps and ecommerce by default.
- Do not retain sensitive health, patient, child or financial records in the inquiry email process.
- Original final deliverables transfer according to signed agreement and full payment; stock fonts, licensed imagery, third-party software, and reusable MediSyncD methods/tools remain subject to their respective terms.
- Deposits, cancellations, refunds, taxes, warranties and dispute clauses must follow final signed agreement reviewed for Missouri law.
- Do not publicly claim that any portfolio demonstration was delivered to a real client.
